## What I’m Building
## Product Portfolio: AI for Government and Crisis Response

Six products along the government work lifecycle: plan and fund, find and win, staff, operate, and respond. 
Each one starts from a real user problem, and makes one clear product decision.

| # | Project | Description | Demo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **FedReq**<br>*Plan and fund* | Govt Project Planner Platform for Govt Employees. Federal acquisition teams draft requirements without seeing whether the money exists, and finance sees them too late. FedReq closes that gap. It is a procurement-to-budget platform that connects guided requirement drafting and historical cost estimation directly to live funds availability across Agency, Program, and Office, and suggests reprogramming options automatically. Unfunded commitments and expiring year-end funds are caught before a package reaches contracting. The key product decision was trust: in a compliance-heavy environment, every recommendation is transparent, explainable, and governed by role across requestors, program managers, contracting officers, and the CFO. | [Live demo](https://fedreqdemo.lovable.app) |
| 2 | **Standup AI**<br>*Find and win* | Small businesses lose hours searching government sites and guessing which bids are worth pursuing. Standup AI turns that into a decision workflow. From a company website and public USAspending data, it builds a profile of what the company does and where it has won before, then scores it against open opportunities from SAM.gov, SBIR.gov, and Grants.gov. For each one, it explains the fit, names the gaps, and recommends what to do about them (for example, team with a subcontractor that has FedRAMP authorization). It also estimates contract value, likely profit, and whether the bid is worth the cost, and tracks every pursuit from Not Started through Awarded. I validated the full flow (profile, match, analyze, estimate, pursue) as a clickable demo before any backend work. | [Live demo](https://standupai.lovable.app) |
| 3 | **GovTalent HR**<br>*Staff: rules first* | Generic HR suites break down in civil-service agencies, where classification structures, collective bargaining agreements, and chronic vacancies drive every decision. GovTalent HR is a position management and labor relations platform built for that reality. The central design choice was to keep anything that must be exact (CBA pay calculations, staffing budget controls, title crosswalks) in a deterministic rules engine, and to use AI only where it can ground every job description, legal risk scan, and negotiation brief in traceable source records. The outcome is a defensible system of record that shortens time-to-hire and contract analysis while reducing grievance exposure and open-records audit risk. | [Live demo](https://govtalen-hr.lovable.app) |
| 4 | **FedHR**<br>*Staff: evidence first* | Federal hiring is slow and succession risk stays hidden, and in this setting AI is only useful if reviewers can trust it. FedHR is an HR copilot and workforce intelligence platform for agencies working within civil service regulations, OPM classification standards, and collective bargaining agreements. It pairs deterministic compensation and workforce modeling with citation-gated AI that drafts audit-ready job descriptions, benchmarks MOUs, and flags single points of failure across agency missions. A strict no-citation, no-claim rule is the core product bet: every recommendation carries an evidence trail that holds up to union scrutiny, legal review, and Section 508 standards. | [Live demo](https://canvas-spark-studio-89.lovable.app) |
| 5 | **MilLogAI**<br>*Operate and sustain* | MilLogAI is a rebuild of a 2017 project using what AI can do today. Logistics soldiers don’t lack information; they lack a way through it. The answer is spread across regulations, manuals, and several systems, and nothing connects them. A supply sergeant with a broken vehicle shouldn’t have to know which document to open first. The demo is built around one story: a vehicle goes down on Day 14, and every screen follows that thread from fault to manual step to part to regulation to shipping check. Three principles guided the design: show the reasoning (every answer cites the exact paragraph and its confidence), keep a human in charge (the AI drafts and a named person approves), and make the rules visible (users can see and toggle the logic behind every verdict). Beyond today’s tools, Audit Shield catches inspection problems early, and the Living Property Book scores confidence for each item so soldiers verify only what matters. All data is synthetic and labeled as such. | [Live demo](https://millogai.lovable.app) |
| 6 | **Harbor Stay**<br>*Respond in a crisis* | When a disaster hits, families need housing within days, and the supply is rarely ready. Harbor Stay is built around speed. Its command center tracks wildfires, floods, storms, and other local disasters as an early warning system, then turns each alert into action: it finds nearby vacant homes, drafts landlord outreach for staff review, and shows supply against expected demand so homes are ready before families call. Around it sits a furnished temporary-housing platform with a short intake, live video tours, and instant booking for 1 to 12 month stays, plus AI-assisted listings for landlords and a tenant portal after move-in. The command center is the differentiator Airbnb and Zillow lack, because it lets Harbor Stay respond to a disaster instead of reacting to it. | [Live demo](https://harbor-home-help.lovable.app/) |
